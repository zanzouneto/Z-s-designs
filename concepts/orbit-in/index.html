The design

Palm Springs modern, the real thing

Orbit In is a 1950s courtyard hotel designed by Herbert Burns. Inside, the rooms are furnished with classic mid-century pieces:

Eames · Saarinen · Nelson · Bertoia · Noguchi · Paulin · Blue Dot · Cortez · Schultz

Kevin Miller and Jinny Park first came as guests, and they’ve owned and run the hotel since 2007.
